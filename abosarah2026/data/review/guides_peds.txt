:wide Neonatal distress
:cell 62
|= Clue | RDS (HMD) | TTN | PPHN | MAS
| Typical baby | **Preterm**, often infant of **diabetic mother** | **Term**, often **C-section** | Term / **post-term**; often 2° to another lung insult (e.g. MAS) | **Post-term** / meconium-stained fluid
| Exam | Grunting, nasal flaring, retractions, **severe** distress | **Tachypnea**, mild distress, cyanosis | **Severe cyanosis**, poor response to O2, ==pre/post-ductal sat gap== | Respiratory distress + desaturation, **meconium staining**
| CXR | **Ground-glass** / fine reticulogranular pattern, **air bronchograms** | **Fluid in fissures**, prominent vascular markings | Often **normal lungs** (shunt is the problem, not lung tissue) | **Patchy infiltrates**, hyperinflation, atelectasis; flattened diaphragms, increased AP diameter
| Cause | **Surfactant deficiency** (lung immaturity) = Hyaline membrane disease | **Delayed clearance** / absorption of fetal lung fluid | **Right-to-left shunting** through PDA / foramen ovale | Meconium plugging airways → obstruction, chemical pneumonitis
| Treatment | **Surfactant** (treatment); **antenatal steroids** to mother (prevention) | Supportive; O2. RR <60 → oral feeding · ==RR 60–80 → NGT== · RR >80 → IV | **Inhaled nitric oxide** | Supportive / O2 if mild; **intubate & ventilate** only if severe
| Deterioration / complication clue | — | O2 sat **not improving despite O2** = worsening, not just symptom duration | — | Can develop **PPHN** as a complication

# 1 كيف أفرّق الـ{respiratory distress} بسرعة؟
> الرئة داخل الرحم مليانة **{fetal lung fluid}**. الـ{vaginal delivery} يعصرها ويطلعها، فمع الـ**{C-section}** يبقى منها شوي ← {tachypnea} يروح خلال يوم إلى ثلاثة = {TTN}.
> الـ**{preterm}** رئته ناقصها {surfactant} (المادة اللي تمنع الـ{alveoli} تنطبق) ← {RDS}. والـ{post-term} اللي تعب قبل الولادة يطلّع {meconium} ويستنشقه ← {MAS}.
> {PPHN}: الـ{CXR} طبيعي، بس الدم **ما يمر على الرئة** ({right-to-left shunt}). عشان كذا {severe cyanosis} ما يتحسن مع {O2}، والـ{saturation} في اليد اليمنى أعلى من الرجل. تشخيصه {Echo}، وعلاجه {inhaled nitric oxide}.
flow: Newborn with respiratory distress -> Gestational age? Mode of delivery? | Preterm: Ground-glass -> RDS \ Surfactant | Term + C-section: Fluid in fissures -> TTN \ Supportive | Post-term + meconium: Patchy infiltrates -> MAS \ Supportive | Cyanosis not improving on O2: Normal CXR -> PPHN \ Nitric oxide
!warn انتبهي: {scaphoid abdomen} + bowel in the chest = {CDH} ← {intubate} على طول، [no:{bag-mask} ممنوع] لأنه ينفخ الأمعاء اللي بالصدر. و{fever} أو أم {GBS positive} = {sepsis} ← {ampicillin + gentamicin}.
%% Term baby by C-section, tachypnea, fluid in the fissures. Cause? → Slow absorption of lung fluid ✔ || {C-section} + {fluid in fissures} = الرئة ما انعصرت = {TTN}.
# 2 الـ{IUGR}: {symmetrical} ولا {asymmetrical}؟
> **{Symmetrical}** = الـ{head circumference} والجسم صغار مع بعض ← المشكلة **من أول الحمل** وفي الـ{fetus} نفسه: {infection} ({TORCH}) أو {chromosomal}. **{Asymmetrical}** = الـ{head circumference} طبيعي والجسم نحيف ← المشكلة **آخر الحمل** من الـ{placenta} ({placental insufficiency})، والجسم "حمى" المخ ({head sparing}).
!mem Complications بعد الولادة: {hypoglycemia} (أهمها) · {hypothermia} · {polycythemia} · {hypocalcemia} ← أول شي {blood glucose}.
# 3 Neonatal jaundice
> كل {newborn} عنده {RBCs} كثيرة، وبعد الولادة تتكسر الزايدة ويطلع {bilirubin}. والـ{liver} لسا بطيء، فيتجمع = {physiological jaundice}، ويبدأ بعد يومين أو ثلاثة. عشان كذا **التوقيت هو كل شي**:
flow: Neonatal jaundice -> When did it start? | First 24 hours: Hemolysis \ ABO / Rh incompatibility -> Mother & baby blood group + Coombs | Day 2–7, well baby: Physiological -> Phototherapy by level | After 1 week, breastfed, well: Breast milk jaundice -> Continue breastfeeding | More than 2 weeks: Check conjugated bilirubin -> Pale stool + dark urine = Biliary atresia \ Kasai before 60 days
* **Phototherapy failed** (indirect bilirubin still very high, e.g. >300) → **Exchange transfusion**
!why ليش ما نوقف الـ{breastfeeding}؟: لأن {breast milk jaundice} ما يضر. أما لو ما يرضع زين أول أسبوع فهذا {breastfeeding jaundice} (قلة {intake}) ← زيدي الرضعات.
%% Newborn 5 days, lethargic, deeply jaundiced. Siblings had the same and needed exchange. Most important to obtain? → Mother's blood group ✔ || الإخوان صار لهم نفس الشي = سبب يتكرر = {isoimmunization} (اختلاف {blood group} الأم مع عيالها).
# 4 Infant of diabetic mother
> {glucose} الأم العالي يعدي للـ{fetus}، فيطلّع {insulin} كثير. بعد الولادة ينقطع {glucose} الأم والـ{insulin} لسا عالي ← **{hypoglycemia}**.
flow: Infant of diabetic mother + jittery -> Check blood glucose -> Low? | Asymptomatic: Oral / breast feeding | Symptomatic (jittery, seizure): IV dextrose
# 5 Resuscitation & birth injuries
> في الـ{neonatal resuscitation} أهم شي **{ventilation}**، لأن الـ{bradycardia} عنده غالبًا من {hypoxia} مو من القلب.
flow: Warm, dry, stimulate -> Not breathing or HR < 100 -> PPV (ventilation) -> HR < 60 after 30 sec of effective PPV -> Chest compressions 3:1
|= Injury | How to tell | Answer
| Caput succedaneum | Present at birth, **crosses** suture lines | Reassure
| Cephalohematoma | Appears hours later, **does not cross** suture lines | Reassure (may cause jaundice)
| Erb palsy (C5–C6) | "Waiter's tip" arm, **grasp reflex present** | Physiotherapy
| Clavicle fracture | Crepitus + asymmetric Moro | No treatment needed
!mem للحفظ: {Moro} و{rooting} يختفون حوالي 4–6 months · {parachute} يظهر 8–9 months.

## Key facts
* **Supplemental iron** for all infants at **4 months**
* Exclusive breastfeeding is **NOT enough** after **6 months**
* **IDA screening** for all infants at **9–12 months**
* **Refeeding syndrome:** hypophosphatemia, hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia
* Liver cirrhosis **without** encephalopathy → restrict **fat** · **with** encephalopathy → restrict **protein**
:end

## Live attenuated vaccines
* **CONTRAINDICATED** in immunodeficiency (or infants likely to develop it)
* MMR · Varicella · Yellow fever · Nasal influenza · BCG · Rotavirus
* **NEVER** give varicella vaccine below **1 year** → give **IVIG** if indicated
* At age **50**: everyone gets the **recombinant shingles vaccine**
## Vaccines in pregnancy
|= Vaccine | Timing | Key note
| Influenza | Anytime (preferably early) | All pregnant women
| Tdap | **28–36 weeks** | Protects newborn (passive antibodies)
| Rubella (MMR) | **Before pregnancy only** | Live → never during pregnancy
:cell 30
## Meningitis prophylaxis
* For **Meningococcal** and **Haemophilus influenzae** contacts, any one of:
* **Rifampin PO** twice a day for 2 days (**preferred**)
* **Ceftriaxone IM** single dose
* **Ciprofloxacin PO** single dose
* No prophylaxis for streptococcus exposure
## Cat bite (rabies)
flow: Cat bite -> ① Wound irrigation immediately -> ② Rabies immunoglobulin -> ③ Inactivated rabies vaccine
## HPV vaccine doses
* **<14 years: 2 doses** (0, then 6–12 months)
* **>14 years: 3 doses** (0, 1–2 months, 6 months)
* Missed a dose → **give the next dose**, never restart
:end

## Asthma: admission & intubation red flags
* Any **ONE** = red flag:
* **SpO2 <92% despite high-flow O2** (92% in pediatrics, 90% in adults)
* ==Normo-capnia== : CO2 should be LOW in an attack; **normal CO2 = impending respiratory failure**
* **Confusion** or any CNS symptoms (coma, low GCS)
* **Bradycardia** or arrhythmia
* **Bradypnea**
* **Failure of initial management**
:cell 30

| GA 29–32 weeks, none of the above | Reassure (no palivizumab)
| GA >32 weeks | Reassure (no palivizumab)
